## Full Description

Press is a curated bookshelf of Christian books. It is a place to discover books on theology, Scripture, prayer, church history, and the Christian life.

Press does not sell books. It helps people find them.

Features:

- Browse 14 carefully selected books across multiple traditions
- Read detailed descriptions, publication information, and scripture references
- Search by title, author, or topic
- Explore collections organized by theme
- Follow reading lists designed for different stages of the Christian life
- Switch between light, dark, and sepia reading themes
- Enable reading mode for a focused, distraction-free experience
- Adjust font size, line height, and letter spacing for comfortable reading
- Full accessibility support with screen reader compatibility

Press is built by Palmshed, a small project exploring how shared digital infrastructure can serve focused applications.
