# Privacy Policy

**Press** — A bookshelf of Christian books

Last updated: July 2026

## Overview

Press is a static bookshelf application. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Data Collection

Press does not collect any data. There is:

- No analytics
- No tracking
- No user accounts
- No cloud sync
- No advertising
- No third-party SDKs that collect data

## Network Usage

Press loads only its own static content. Book cover images and page content are served from the app bundle. The app requires an internet connection only to load availability links (Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Google Books, Christianbook) which are opened in your browser. These sites have their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

Press requests the following permissions:

- **Internet** (Android): Required to load availability links in your browser. Press does not transmit any data.

Press does not request access to:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos or media
- Device identifiers
- Biometric data

## Children's Privacy

Press does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be available in the app repository at [github.com/bniladridas/press](https://github.com/bniladridas/press).

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at [github.com/bniladridas/press/issues](https://github.com/bniladridas/press/issues).
